.. important::

   The MongoDB Connector for Business Intelligence for |service| and on-premises reaches end-of-life (EOL) and will no longer be supported after September 2026.
   For all new projects, we recommend using the new {+sql-interface-full+} to
   connect to |service| or Enterprise Advanced deployments.
   The {+sql-interface+} provides a simpler, direct connection to your
   {+clusters+} without requiring a separate BI Connector instance, and
   it offers improved performance, a simplified setup, and enhanced
   features. To learn more, see :ref:`connect-with-sql-overview`.
