.. note::

   Clusters hosted on |aws| make a distinction between **Standard IOPS** and
   **Provisioned or High Performance IOPS**. This distinction stems from the
   cluster's underlying SSD volumes, and is exclusive to clusters hosted on
   |aws|.

   To learn more, see :ref:`Provisioned IOPS and High Performance IOPS <provisioned-high-perf-iops>`.
