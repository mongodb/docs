======================================
FAQ: Account Access and Authentication
======================================

.. meta::
   :description: Find answers to common questions about accessing your Atlas account, including password issues, multi-factor authentication, SSO, and account deletion.

Why is my password invalid?
---------------------------

Your |service| user account password must comply with the
following requirements:

- Contain at least eight characters.
- Exclude your email address and |service| username.
- Differ from your previous 24 passwords.
- Differ from the most commonly used passwords.

If your password doesn't meet these requirements, |service|
prompts you to create a new one. For password reset instructions,
see :ref:`atlas-faq-access-password-reset`.

.. _atlas-faq-access-password-reset:

How do I reset my password?
---------------------------

The method for resetting your password depends on how you
registered:

- If you registered with your email address, use the
  |service-login| to reset your password.
- If you registered with Google, Google manages your credentials.
  Reset your password through your Google account.
- If you registered with GitHub, GitHub manages your credentials.
  Reset your password through your GitHub account.

Why didn't I receive a verification email?
------------------------------------------

Verification emails come from the ``mongodb.com`` domain. If you
don't receive a verification email, verify that your email
provider accepts mail from ``mongodb.com``. If the issue
persists, contact |mdb-support|.

Why can't I register with my GitHub account?
--------------------------------------------

|service| requires your GitHub account to have a public email
address. If you try to register without a public email address,
|service| returns an error.

To set a public GitHub email address, see
:ref:`github-accounts`.

How do I link or unlink my Google or GitHub account?
----------------------------------------------------

You can link or unlink your |service| account from your Google or
GitHub account at any time:

- To unlink from GitHub, see
  :ref:`unlink-your-mongodb-account-from-github`.
- To unlink from Google, see
  :ref:`unlink-your-mongodb-account-from-google`.

How do I set up multi-factor authentication?
--------------------------------------------

|service| uses |mfa| to verify your identity at login. You can
set up many authentication methods, including security keys,
Okta Verify, authenticator apps, or email.
Configure at least two methods to avoid losing access to your
account.

.. include:: /includes/fact-2fa-retired.rst

To learn how to enable and manage |mfa|, see
:ref:`atlas-enable-mfa`.

What do I do if I lose access to multi-factor authentication?
-------------------------------------------------------------

If you lose access to all your |mfa| methods and can't log in,
contact |mdb-support|.

To avoid being locked out, set up at least two |mfa| methods. To
learn more, see :ref:`atlas-enable-mfa`.

Why can't I use my old email address after deleting my account?
---------------------------------------------------------------

|service| requires up to two weeks to process an account
deletion. During that time, the email address associated with
your deleted account is reserved and unavailable for a new
account registration.

After the deletion completes, you can register a new account
with the same email address. To learn more, see
:ref:`delete-atlas-account`.
