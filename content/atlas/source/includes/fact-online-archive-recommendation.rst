On {+atlas-core-cluster+}s, you can configure :ref:`{+Online-Archive+}
<online-archive-overview>` to move infrequently accessed data from your
|service| cluster to a MongoDB-managed read-only {+fdi+} instead of
sharding your collection or upgrading your cluster tier. On
{+atlas-infinite-cluster+}s, {+Online-Archive+} is not supported in
public preview. To learn more about {+Online-Archive+}, see
:ref:`manage-online-archive`.
