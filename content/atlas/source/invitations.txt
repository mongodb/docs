.. _view-invitations:

=========================================
Invitations to Organizations and Projects
=========================================

.. meta::
   :description: Manage invitations to organizations and projects in Atlas, including viewing and accepting pending invitations.

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

|service| grants users access to organizations and projects through
invitations. When an administrator adds you to an |service|
organization or project, |service| sends you an invitation. Your
membership remains pending until you accept the invitation.
Invitations expire after 30 days.

Use this page to view and accept invitations that you receive and to
view pending invitations that your organization or project sends. To
learn how to invite users, see :ref:`atlas-org-users` and
:ref:`manage-project-access`.

.. include:: /includes/fact-project-invite-deprecation.rst

.. _inviting-federated-users:

Inviting Federated Users
------------------------

In organizations that use :ref:`federated authentication
<atlas-federated-authentication>`, |service| adds invited federated
users directly to organizations and projects as active members when
both of the following conditions are true:

- The invited user's email address belongs to a verified domain that
  is mapped to an active |idp| configuration.

- The organization is connected to your federation through the same
  |idp| configuration.

These users don't receive an invitation and don't need to accept one.
Users who don't meet these conditions continue to receive standard
invitations.

Organization and project owners add users through the same invite
workflow. Federated users added directly appear as active members
immediately instead of appearing as :guilabel:`Pending User`.
|service| sends these users a notification email that doesn't require
acceptance.

If a federated user's invitation is pending, expired, or rejected,
re-adding the user adds them directly as an active member.

If an invited user doesn't have an |service| account, their first
and last name appear after their first login.

Accept Invitations to Join Organizations
----------------------------------------

The :guilabel:`Invitations` tab of your |service| account lists your
pending invitations to join |service| organizations.

To view pending invitations to join organizations:

.. include:: /includes/steps/view-invitations.rst

To accept an invitation, click :guilabel:`Accept Invite`
for the corresponding invitation.

You can also accept an invitation from the email |service| sends when
you are first invited to join an organization or project.

Invitation Details
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

|service| displays the following information for each invitation:

.. list-table::
   :widths: 20 60

   * - Organization
     - Name of the organization you are invited to join.

   * - Roles
     - :ref:`User roles <user-roles>` this invitation grants on the
       specified organization and projects.

   * - Sent On
     - Time that the invitation was sent.

   * - Expiration Date
     - Date that the invitation expires.

   * - Actions
     - Button to accept the invitation.

View Outgoing Invitations
-------------------------

Organization and project owners can view invitations that are
awaiting a response.

To view outgoing invitations to an organization:

.. include:: /includes/steps-view-outgoing-invitations-org.rst

To view outgoing invitations to a project:

.. include:: /includes/steps-view-outgoing-invitations-project.rst

|service| displays outgoing invitations as :guilabel:`Pending User`
until the recipient accepts the invitation. |service| adds eligible
:ref:`federated users <inviting-federated-users>` directly as active
members instead. Their first and last names don't populate until
their first login.
