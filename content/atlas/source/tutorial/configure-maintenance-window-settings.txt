=========================================
Configure Maintenance Management Settings
=========================================

.. meta::
   :keywords: atlas cli, atlas ui
   :description: Configure the hour for Atlas to start weekly maintenance on your cluster, with options for maintenance windows, protected hours, maintenance waves, and deleting your maintenance configuration.

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. facet::
   :name: genre
   :values: tutorial

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

Use the following procedures to configure and delete the maintenance
windows, protected hours, and maintenance waves for the projects in
your organization. To learn more, see :ref:`configure-maintenance-window`.

To manage maintenance windows, you must have
:authrole:`Organization Owner` or :authrole:`Project Owner` access
to the project.

   Configure Your Maintenance Management Settings
   ----------------------------------------------

   .. selected-content::
      :selections: atlas-ui

      You can configure a project's maintenance window, protected hours, and
      maintenance waves from your organization's settings or from that
      project's settings.

      .. tabs::

         .. tab:: Organization Settings
            :tabid: org
                  
            To configure maintenance waves for all your projects from your
            organization's settings using the {+atlas-ui+}:

            .. procedure::
               :style: normal

               .. include:: /includes/nav/steps-org-settings.rst

               .. step:: Navigate to the :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section.
               
                  The :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section displays
                  the maintenance windows and waves you have configured for the
                  projects in your organization.

               .. step:: Click :guilabel:`Edit waves for all projects`.

                  You can either manually assign your projects maintenance
                  waves or use project tags to automatically assign
                  maintenance waves.

               .. step:: Manually Assign Maintenance Waves to your Projects.
               
                  To manually assign maintenance waves to your projects, select
                  :guilabel:`Manual Assignment`.

                  1. Use the dropdown next to each project to assign a maintenance
                     wave.

                  #. If you have environment project tags set for your projects,
                     you can use the
                     :guilabel:`Generate waves based on existing project tags`
                     option.

                     .. include:: /includes/list-tables/project-tags-maintenance-wave.rst

                     |service| **does not** automatically update these assignments if
                     you update a project's environment tag.

                  If you have :guilabel:`Manual Assignment` selected, you can
                  also configure a project's maintenance wave as you
                  configure its maintenance window.

               .. step:: Link Maintenance Waves to your Projects' Environment Tags
            
                  Instead of manually assigning maintenance waves to your
                  projects, you can link your projects' maintenance waves to
                  their environment project tags.
                  
                  Select :guilabel:`Use Project Tags to auto-define waves`.

                  .. include:: /includes/list-tables/project-tags-maintenance-wave.rst

                  |service| automatically updates these assignments as you update
                  a project's environment tag.

               .. step:: Click :guilabel:`Save` to keep your changes or click :guilabel:`Cancel` to discard them.

            To configure select projects' maintenance settings using the
            {+atlas-ui+}:

            .. procedure::
               :style: normal

               .. include:: /includes/nav/steps-org-settings.rst

               .. step:: Navigate to the :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section.
               
                  The :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section displays
                  the maintenance windows and waves you have configured for the
                  projects in your organization.

               .. step:: Select one or more projects whose maintenance window you want to configure.

                  To edit the maintenance window of a single project, click the
                  :icon-fa5:`pencil-alt` next to the project to open the
                  :guilabel:`Edit Maintenance Window` page.

                  To edit the maintenance window of multiple projects, click
                  the checkbox next to the projects you want to select.
                  Then, click :guilabel:`Edit maintenance window`.

               .. step:: Set a Maintenance Window.
               
                  Set the day and time that you want |service| to start
                  maintenance on the projects you selected.

               .. step:: Set Protected Hours.

                  To set a range of protected hours in which |service| avoids
                  performing standard updates, toggle the
                  :guilabel:`Enable protected hours` setting to :guilabel:`On`.
                  You can set a maximum time range of 18 hours a day.

               .. step:: (Optional) Turn on Auto-defer.

                  If you want |service| to automatically defer any scheduled
                  maintenance for one week, click
                  :guilabel:`Automatically defer maintenance for one week`.
                  Enabling this option configures automatic deferral for future
                  maintenance windows. To learn more, see
                  :ref:`auto-defer-maintenance`.
                  
                  To defer currently scheduled maintenance, use the
                  :guilabel:`Defer 1 Week` button.

                  .. include:: /includes/admonitions/importants/maintenance-waves-auto-deferrals.rst

               .. step:: Assign a Maintenance Wave.

                  To set the maintenance waves, use the dropdown under
                  :guilabel:`Wave (per project)` to assign your project
                  or projects to a maintenance wave.

                  .. include:: /includes/admonitions/notes/maint-waves-with-proj-tags.rst

               .. step:: Click :guilabel:`Save` to keep your changes or click :guilabel:`Cancel` to discard them.

         .. tab:: Project Settings
            :tabid: project

            .. procedure::
               :style: normal

               .. include:: /includes/nav/steps-project-settings.rst

                  The :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section displays
                  the maintenance window and wave you have configured for a
                  project. To configure maintenance settings from a projects's
                  settings using the {+atlas-ui+}:

               .. step:: Click the :guilabel:`Edit maintenance window` button.

               .. step:: Set a Maintenance Window.
                  
                  Set the day and time that you want |service| to start
                  maintenance on your project.

               .. step:: Set Protected Hours.
               
                  To set a range of protected hours in which |service| avoids
                  performing standard updates, toggle the
                  :guilabel:`Enable protected hours` setting to :guilabel:`On`.
                  You can set a maximum time range of 18 hours a day.

               .. step:: (Optional) Turn on Auto-defer.
               
                  If you want |service| to automatically defer any scheduled
                  maintenance for one week, click
                  :guilabel:`Automatically defer maintenance for one week`.
                  Enabling this option configures automatic deferral for future
                  maintenance windows. To learn more, see
                  :ref:`auto-defer-maintenance`.
               
                  To defer currently scheduled maintenance, use the
                  :guilabel:`Defer 1 Week` button.

                  .. include:: /includes/admonitions/importants/maintenance-waves-auto-deferrals.rst

               .. step:: Assign a Maintenance Wave.
                  
                  To set the maintenance wave, use the dropdown under
                  :guilabel:`Wave (per project)` to assign your project
                  or projects to a maintenance wave.

                  .. include:: /includes/admonitions/notes/maint-waves-with-proj-tags.rst

               .. step:: Click :guilabel:`Save` to keep your changes or click :guilabel:`Cancel` to discard them.

   Delete Your Maintenance Management Settings
   -------------------------------------------

   If you have maintenance windows, protected hours, or maintenance
   waves configured, you can delete these settings. Deleting your
   maintenance window configuration restores the default maintenance
   window settings.

   .. selected-content::
      :selections: atlas-ui

      .. tabs::

         .. tab:: Organization Settings
            :tabid: org

            You can delete all configured maintenance waves by clicking
            :guilabel:`Delete waves for all projects` in the
            :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section.

            To delete specific maintenance settings:

            .. procedure::
               :style: normal

               .. step:: If you have maintenance waves configured, click the
                  tab corresponding to the wave of the project whose maintenance
                  window you want to delete.

               .. step:: Under :guilabel:`Actions`, click the :icon-fa5:`trash-alt`
                  icon next to the maintenance window you want to delete. This
                  deletes the maintenance window, protected hours, and the
                  assigned wave.

               .. step:: To delete an assigned maintenance waves without
                  deleting the maintenance window:

                  1. Select one or more projects whose maintenance waves
                     you want to delete.

                     To delete the maintenance wave of a single project, click
                     the :icon-fa5:`pencil-alt` next to the project to open the
                     :guilabel:`Edit Maintenance Window` page.

                     To delete the maintenance waves of multiple projects,
                     click the checkbox next to the projects you want to
                     select. Then, click :guilabel:`Edit Maintenance Window`.

                  #. Select :guilabel:`None` using the dropdown under
                     :guilabel:`Wave (per project)`.

         .. tab:: Project Settings
            :tabid: project

            To delete the configured maintenance settings using the
            {+atlas-ui+}, click the :icon-fa5:`trash-alt` icon in the
            :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section.

            To delete an assigned maintenance wave without deleting the
            maintenance window:

            .. procedure::
               :style: normal

               .. step:: Click :guilabel:`Edit` in the
                  :guilabel:`Maintenance Window and Waves` section.

               .. step:: Select :guilabel:`None` using the dropdown under
                  :guilabel:`Wave (per project)`.

               .. step:: Click :guilabel:`Save` to keep your changes or click
                  :guilabel:`Cancel` to discard them.
