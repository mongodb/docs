==================================================
Manage Domain Mapping for Federated Authentication
==================================================

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. meta::
   :keywords: connect, federated authentication
   :description: Map domains to your Identity Provider in Atlas to streamline federated authentication, control which users can log in through the IdP, and manage domain verification methods.

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

You can map domains to your |idp| to streamline the login experience
for users from specified domains by authenticating them through an
|idp|. Domain mapping ensures that all users with a particular domain
in their email address have the same login experience.

Domain mapping also limits the users that can authenticate through
your |idp|. |service| denies access to users whose email addresses use
unmapped domains, even if the |idp| authenticates them. This behavior
applies regardless of the :ref:`Restrict Access by Domain
<restrict-org-access-by-domain>` setting.

To grant access to a user on an unmapped domain, map that domain to
your |idp| with the procedure on this page. If your organization
doesn't own the domain, :ref:`invite the user through the standard
flow <inviting-federated-users>` instead.

.. include:: /includes/admonitions/use-alternate-idp-multiple-mapped-domains.rst

To map a domain to your |idp|, you must verify that you own the
domain. You can either:

- Upload an |html| file containing a verification key to a host in your
  domain or

- Create a |dns| :wikipedia:`TXT record <TXT_record?oldid=930320014>`
  that contains a verification key.

.. include:: /includes/roles-required-fed-auth.rst
