agentengine service-account delete
----------------------------------

Deactivate a service account.

Synopsis
~~~~~~~~

Deactivate a service account.

Deactivation takes effect on the next request: existing access tokens
are rejected and the credential can no longer mint new ones. No command
reactivates an account; create a replacement instead.

.. code-block:: bash

   agentengine service-account delete <client-id> [flags]

~~~~~~~
