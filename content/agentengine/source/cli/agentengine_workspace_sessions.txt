agentengine workspace sessions
------------------------------

List and stop runtime sessions for a workspace.

Synopsis
~~~~~~~~

List and stop sessions that currently hold reserved runtime for a
workspace.

A session is active while it holds reserved agent and tool sandbox
capacity, and stopping while that capacity is still releasing. It
disappears from the list once released. Conversation history remains
after the backing runtime is gone.

Testing sessions created from the Playground or ``agentengine invoke``
may be reclaimed automatically when the workspace is at capacity. Other
sessions are user-managed and stay reserved until they go idle or you
stop them.

Examples:

.. code-block:: bash

    agentengine workspace sessions list
    agentengine workspace sessions stop <session-id>
