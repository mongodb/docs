=========================================
Manage Data Collection for Model Training
=========================================

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. meta::
   :description: Learn where to opt in to or opt out of data collection for Voyage AI model training from the Organization Settings page in Atlas.
   :keywords: Atlas, Voyage AI, model training, opt out, opt in

.. facet::
   :name: genre
   :values: reference

MongoDB can collect the data that you send to the {+voyage-api-full+}
and use it to improve {+voyageai+} models. You manage this setting for
your organization from the :guilabel:`Organization Settings` page in
the {+atlas-ui+}.

To learn about eligibility and the steps to opt in or opt out, see
:atlas:`Manage Data Collection for Voyage AI Model Training
</tutorial/manage-organization-settings/#manage-data-collection-for-voyage-ai-model-training>`.
