The {+mdb-ai-platform+} is the enterprise-grade platform for developing,
deploying, and managing the full lifecycle of AI agents. The {+platform-short+}
replaces the need for multiple vendors such as vector databases, orchestration
platforms, runtime, and synchronization pipelines. Sandboxing and agent identity
management are built into the platform so that regulated workloads stay isolated
and auditable on every run. You can apply the full stack or one layer of the
platform, and you can run on your Atlas clusters.

How the {+platform-short+} is Unique
------------------------------------

- **Open and flexible**: Deploy any model or framework across any cloud or
  on-premises environment. Build with open-source frameworks like LangGraph
  while MongoDB handles scaling, provisioning, state management, and operational
  complexity.

- **Strong security, governance, and isolation at scale**: Observe, govern, and
  sandbox agentic workflows with complete visibility and cost controls. Maintain
  compliance with GDPR, SOC 2, HIPAA, and industry-specific requirements.

- **Advanced Agentic Memory and State for more efficient and accurate agents**:
  Automatically manage session state and long-term memory by using integrated
  vector storage by MongoDB.

- **Designed for enterprise integration**: Connect legacy and third-party
  tools by using open standards and real-time vector indexing.

Responsibilities
----------------

{+platform-short+} users are responsible for the behavior and actions of their
agents. Users are responsible for ensuring that their agents comply with
MongoDB's `Acceptable Use Policy
<https://www.mongodb.com/legal/acceptable-use-policy>`__ and `Terms of Service
<https://www.mongodb.com/legal/terms-and-conditions/cloud>`__. Use the
:ref:`Agent Engine Policy Engine <agentic-platform-policy-engine>` to manage and
control agent execution.
