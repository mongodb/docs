.. important:: 

   Do not choose :guilabel:`Latest Version With Auto Upgrades`. 
   This option auto upgrades your cluster to the latest minor release. 
   Some minor releases, such as MongoDB version 8.2, may not support
   Live Migration or {+c2c-product-name+}. When
   :ref:`upgrading <upgrade-major-MongoDB-version>`, choose a major
   version to ensure compatibility with Live Migration and
   {+c2c-product-name+}.
