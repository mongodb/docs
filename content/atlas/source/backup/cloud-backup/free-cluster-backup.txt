========================
{+Free-Cluster+} Backups
========================

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

.. include:: /includes/infinite/banner-same-as-core.rst

|service| offers an optional backup subscription for
{+Free-clusters+} (``M0``). When you enable backups for a
{+Free-cluster+}, |service| takes daily snapshots of the cluster and
retains the last four snapshots.

|service| bills $0.10 per day for each {+Free-cluster+} with backups
enabled, up to a maximum of $3.00 per month per cluster. To learn
more, see :ref:`free-cluster-backup-billing`.

.. important::

   Backups for {+Free-clusters+} are available as a private preview.
   The feature and corresponding documentation might change at any
   time during the preview. |service| rolls out the preview gradually,
   so you might not see the option to enable backups for your
   {+Free-cluster+}.

Limitations
-----------

- Custom policies are not supported for {+Free-cluster+} snapshots.
  |service| takes a single daily snapshot at the same time each day.

  If you require finer-grained backups, consider upgrading to an
  ``M10`` or larger cluster tier.

- On-demand snapshots are not supported for {+Free-clusters+}.

- You can't restore {+Free-cluster+} snapshots to a sharded cluster.
  You can only restore {+Free-cluster+} snapshots to replica sets.

Enable Backups for a {+Free-Cluster+}
-------------------------------------

You can enable backups for a {+Free-cluster+} from several places in
the {+atlas-ui+}:

- From the :guilabel:`Backups` section of the project overview, click
  :guilabel:`Add daily backups`.
- From the cluster's :guilabel:`Backup` page, click the option to
  enable backups.
- From the cluster overview, next to the :guilabel:`Backup: Inactive`
  indicator, click the option to enable backups and follow the
  prompts.
- When you create or upgrade a cluster, toggle on :guilabel:`Add Backups`
  in the tier selection screen.

After you enable backups, |service| begins taking daily snapshots and
billing the subscription.

.. note::

   Your organization must have a payment method on file to enable
   backups. If it doesn't, |service| prompts you to add one during
   enablement.

Paused Clusters
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If your {+Free-cluster+} is paused, |service| pauses backups and
subscription billing. When you resume the cluster, backups and billing
resume automatically.

If you delete a paused {+Free-cluster+} that has backups enabled,
|service| deletes the snapshots and cancels the subscription.

View and Restore Snapshots
--------------------------

|service| displays snapshots for your {+Free-cluster+} on the
cluster's :guilabel:`Backup` page. You can restore a snapshot to the
same {+Free-cluster+} or to another {+Free-cluster+} in the same
organization. You can also restore a snapshot to a {+Flex-cluster+}
or {+Dedicated-cluster+} in the same organization, or download a
snapshot to a local machine.

To restore or download a snapshot, see
:ref:`restore-from-snapshot`.

Cancel the Backup Subscription
------------------------------

You can cancel the backup subscription at any time. When you cancel,
|service| immediately stops taking snapshots, deletes existing snapshots,
and stops billing for the subscription.

To cancel the subscription, open the menu on the cluster's
:guilabel:`Backup` page and select the option to cancel. You can also
turn off backups in the cluster configuration's
:guilabel:`Additional Settings` section.

If you delete a {+Free-cluster+} that has backups enabled, |service|
immediately stops taking snapshots and cancels the subscription.
|service| removes your access to existing snapshots and deletes them
within 24 hours.
