===========================================
MongoDB Atlas: Multi-Cloud Database Service
===========================================

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. meta::
   :keywords: overview, atlas core, atlas infinite, editions, compare
   :description: MongoDB Atlas is a fully managed multi-cloud database service that simplifies deploying, managing, and scaling your databases on AWS, Azure, and Google Cloud.

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

|service-fullname| is a multi-cloud database service that simplifies 
deploying and managing your databases while offering the versatility 
you need to build resilient and performant global applications on the 
cloud providers of your choice.

|service| runs your databases on |aws|, |azure|, and |gcp|, and
handles the operational work of running them: provisioning, patching,
backup, monitoring, and scaling. You interact with your data through
the MongoDB Query API, so your application code, drivers, and tools
work the same way that they do against a self-managed deployment.

|service| offers two database editions, {+atlas-core-full+} and
{+atlas-infinite-full+}, which differ in how they manage compute and
storage. To understand the differences and choose between them, 
see :ref:`atlas-editions`.

What You Can Do with Atlas
--------------------------

|service| helps you deploy and manage databases on-demand when
and where you need them. With |service|, you can:

- **Build AI-powered features**: Semantic search, RAG, and agents with
  automatic embeddings all run on your existing operational data.
- **Run transactional workloads at scale**: Flexible document modeling
  and elastic scaling handle both steady growth and traffic spikes.
- **Search and analyze live data where it lives**: Full-text, vector,
  and hybrid search plus real-time analytics run directly on |service|
  data, no separate systems to sync or manage.
- **Deploy anywhere with enterprise-grade security and resilience**: 
  Multi-cloud, multi-region, and built-in compliance controls support
  complex business needs, coupled with automated backups, self-healing,
  and automatic failover.
- **Scale from prototype to production on one platform**: The same APIs,
  drivers, and tools are available all the way from free to
  scaled deployments.

Features
~~~~~~~~
|service| includes a mature feature set of services and capabilities designed
for scalability, integrations, and AI-centric workflows that you
can add as needed without having to deploy and maintain separate systems:

- Build :ref:`full-text search <atlas-search>` and 
  :ref:`Vector Search <avs-overview>` on your operational data so that RAG,
  recommendations, and AI agents work against the same data, indexes, and
  security model as the rest of the app.
- Drive your app's retrieval capacity with :ref:`Voyage AI <voyage-landing>`
  embedding models and APIs that turn data into high-accuracy vectors natively
  in the platform.
- :ref:`Automate embeddings <avs-auto-embeddings-overview>` with no need
  for pipelines or machine learning expertise, generated by MongoDB and updated
  automatically so AI agents can access accurate contextual information
  in real time.
- Orchestrate agents using a precise semantic search interface with 
  :ref:`long-term memory store <langgraph-js-long-term-memory>` integration
  and :ref:`lexical prefilters <fts-vectorSearch-ref>`.
- Use :ref:`Atlas Stream Processing <atlas-sp-overview>` to read, write, and
  transform streams of complex data as it arrives.
