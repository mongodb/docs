===========================================
Estimate Atlas Flex Cluster Costs and Usage
===========================================

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 2
   :class: singlecol

|service| {+Flex-clusters+} give you access to modern database
features including |fts|, {+avs+}, |atlas-sp|, triggers, and more.
To learn more, see :ref:`Cluster Types <atlas-choose-flex>`.

{+Flex-clusters+} are capped at $30 per month. You pay a monthly base price,
which includes:

- 5GB storage
- 100 operations/sec
- unlimited data transfer

{+Flex-clusters+} scale with your usage. If you use additional operations/sec,
MongoDB charges you based on the following pricing tiers.

Usage Cost Summary
------------------

As a user of an {+Atlas-Flex+} {+cluster+}, you pay between $8 and $30 for
30 days of usage. As for all |service| {+clusters+}, the {+Flex-cluster+}
tier follows a pay-as-you-go model billed hourly.

The following table outlines the pricing for {+Flex-clusters+}. Note that
there is a volume discount as your usage increases.

   * - Tier (ops/second)
     - Marginal Monthly Cost
     - Effective Discount
     - Total Monthly Cost
     - Total Hourly Cost

     - $8.00
     - 0%
     - $8.00

   * - 100 - 200
     - $7.00
     - 12.5%
     - $15.00

Estimate Your Monthly Cost
--------------------------

To estimate your monthly {+Flex-cluster+} cost, find your cluster's
current operations per second (ops/sec) usage. Then, compare that
value against the tiers in the preceding table.

.. procedure::
   :style: normal

   .. include:: /includes/nav/steps-db-deployments-page.rst

   .. step:: Open the cluster metrics.

      Click the name of the {+Flex-cluster+} to open the
      :guilabel:`Cluster` sidebar, then click :guilabel:`Metrics`.

   .. step:: Find your Opcounters value.

      Locate the :guilabel:`Opcounters` chart, which shows the
      operations per second that your cluster runs. To learn more
      about this and other available metrics, see
      :ref:`review-flex-metrics`.

Compare the ops/sec value from the :guilabel:`Opcounters` chart
against the tiers in the Usage Cost Summary table to estimate your
marginal and total monthly cost. Your usage can vary throughout the
month, so you might move between tiers as your workload changes, as
the following examples show.

Examples
~~~~~~~~

**Scenario 1 - Monthly Usage**

Your workload runs less than 100 ops/sec for 20 days, and then bursts
up to 250 ops/second for 5 days. Then, for another 3 days, the workload bursts
to 500 ops/second.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Usage
     - Cost

   * - Your workload runs less than 100 ops/sec for 20 days.
     - $0.011/hour * 24 hours * 20 days = $5.28

   * - The workload bursts up to 250 ops/second for 5 days.
     - $0.0288/hour * 24 hours * 5 days = $3.46

   * - The workload bursts to 500 ops/second for another 3 days.
     - $0.0411/hour * 24 hours * 3 days = $2.96

   * - **Total Usage Charge**
     - **$5.28 + $3.46 + $2.96 = $11.70**

**Scenario 2 - One Day Use**

Your workload runs less than 100 ops/sec for 5 hours. It bursts up to
410 ops/second for 10 hours, followed by 9 hours at 150 ops/second.
After these 24 hours, you delete the {+cluster+}.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Usage
     - Cost

   * - Your workload runs less than 100 ops/sec for 5 hours.
     - $0.011 hourly base cost * 5 hours = $0.055

   * - The workload bursts up to 410 ops/second for 10 hours.
     - $0.0411/hour * 10 hours = $0.41

   * - The workload drops to 150 ops/second for another 9 hours.
     - $0.0205/hour * 9 hours = $0.18

   * - You delete the cluster.
     - (no charge)

   * - **Total Usage Charge**
     - **$0.055 + $0.41 + $0.18 = $0.65**

**Scenario 3 - Migrating from a Retired M2 or M5 Tier**

.. include:: /includes/shared-to-atlas-flex-migration.rst

Suppose |service| migrated your ``M2`` or ``M5`` {+cluster+} to a
{+Flex-cluster+}. Before the migration, this {+cluster+} billed at a
flat monthly rate. Now, your cost depends on your workload's ops/sec
usage instead of a fixed price.

Your workload runs less than 100 ops/sec for 25 days, and then
bursts up to 200 ops/second for 5 days.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1

   * - Usage
     - Cost

   * - Your workload runs less than 100 ops/sec for 25 days.
     - $0.011/hour * 24 hours * 25 days = $6.60

   * - The workload bursts up to 200 ops/second for 5 days.
     - $0.0205/hour * 24 hours * 5 days = $2.46

   * - **Total Usage Charge**
     - **$6.60 + $2.46 = $9.06**

To learn more about migrating your applications and tooling from a
retired ``M2`` or ``M5`` {+cluster+}, see :ref:`flex-migration`.

Flex versus M0 or Dedicated Clusters
------------------------------------

Choose the {+cluster+} tier that matches your workload and budget.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 15 25 60

   * - Tier
     - Cost
     - Best For

   * - {+Free-cluster+} (``M0``)
     - No cost
     - Learning MongoDB, prototyping, and other workloads that fit
       within the :ref:`atlas-free-tier`.

   * - {+Flex-cluster+}
     - $8 to $30 per month, based on usage
     - Development, testing, and low-traffic production workloads
       that need modern database features such as |fts|, {+avs+},
       and |atlas-sp|, without managing dedicated infrastructure.

   * - {+Dedicated-cluster+} (``M10+``)
     - Starts at $60 per month
     - Production workloads that need dedicated hardware,
       consistent resources, granular backup configuration, or
       features that {+Flex-clusters+} don't support.

To compare {+Flex-cluster+} and {+Dedicated-cluster+} feature support
in detail, see :ref:`ref-flex-unsupported-features`. If your
{+Flex-cluster+} consistently reaches the throughput or storage
limits described in the next section, consider switching to a
{+Dedicated-cluster+} for dedicated resources and higher limits.

Storage, Throughput, and Data Transfer Limits
---------------------------------------------

{+Flex-clusters+} have usage limits that are separate from the
pricing tiers in this guide. To learn about all {+Flex-cluster+}
limits, see :ref:`flex-cluster-limitations`.

Storage Over 5 GB
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

{+Flex-clusters+} limit total data storage to 5 GB, which includes
all uncompressed BSON documents stored in your collections plus
their associated indexes. If your workload needs more storage,
switch to a {+Dedicated-cluster+}.

Throughput Above 500 Operations/Second
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

The pricing tiers in the Usage Cost Summary table apply only up to
500 operations/second. Above that point, {+Flex-clusters+} enforce a
separate hard limit rather than a higher pricing tier.

|service| triggers a sub-second cooldown period on each connection
before resuming that connection's operations. If the operation
queue exceeds the operations per second limit, operations might wait
in the queue for more than a second.

Data Transfer
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

{+Flex-clusters+} include unlimited data transfer in the base price.
|service| doesn't charge {+Free-clusters+} or {+Flex-clusters+} for
outgoing data transfer. To learn more, see :ref:`data-transfer-costs`.
