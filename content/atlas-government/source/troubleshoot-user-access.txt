===============================
Troubleshoot User Access Issues
===============================

.. meta::
   :description: Troubleshoot MongoDB Atlas for Government user access issues, including login failures, repeated MFA prompts, email verification, federated login, and account suspension.

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 2
   :class: singlecol

.. note::

   This page covers |cloudgov| user accounts. |cloudgov-short| users
   are separate from database users. Database users can access MongoDB
   databases, while |cloudgov-short| users can access the
   |cloudgov-short| application itself.

Use this page to resolve common issues with |cloudgov-short| user
account access.

Prerequisite Checks
-------------------

Before you troubleshoot, confirm the following:

- You log in at the |cloudgov-short| account page:
  ``https://account.mongodbgov.com``.

- .. include:: /includes/facts/sso-login-url.rst

- Your account is not locked or suspended. To learn more about
  lockout and inactivity suspension policies, see
  :ref:`cloudgov-access`.

Common Issues and Resolutions
-----------------------------

Use the following sections to diagnose and resolve common user
access issues.

Login Failures and Account Lockout
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

After 3 failed login attempts, |cloudgov-short| locks your account
for 30 minutes. Wait 30 minutes and try again, or reset your
password from the |cloudgov-short| login page. If you still cannot
log in, contact MongoDB Support. To learn more, see :ref:`support`.

Email Verification Issues
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

|cloudgov-short| sends invitation and verification emails from the
``mongodbgov.com`` domain. If you don't receive an expected email:

.. procedure::
   :style: normal

   .. step:: Check your spam or junk folder.

   .. step:: Confirm the recipient address with your organization
      owner.

      Confirm that |cloudgov-short| sent the invitation to the
      correct email address.

   .. step:: Add the domain to your email allowlist.

      Ask your email administrator to add the ``mongodbgov.com``
      domain to the allowlist so that |cloudgov-short| emails
      reach your inbox.

   .. step:: Request a new invitation or contact MongoDB Support.

      If you still don't receive the email, ask your organization
      owner to resend the invitation, or contact MongoDB Support.
      To learn more, see :ref:`support`.

Repeated MFA Prompts
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

|cloudgov-short| ends your session after 15 minutes of inactivity
and prompts you to log in again, including completing |mfa|.
This is expected behavior.

If |cloudgov-short| prompts you for |mfa| repeatedly within an
active session, or you cannot complete an |mfa| challenge with
your enrolled factors, contact :ref:`MongoDB Support <support>`.

Federated Users Prompted for a Password or MFA Setup
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

If your organization uses federated authentication, you authenticate
through your identity provider. |cloudgov-short| does not prompt
federated users for a password or |mfa| enrollment.

If you see an unexpected prompt to create a password or set up MFA:

.. procedure::
   :style: normal

   .. step:: Don't enter any credentials.

   .. step:: Log in through your organization's SSO URL.

      Go to ``https://account.mongodbgov.com/sso/{idpId}``, where
      ``{idpId}`` is your organization's identity provider ID.

   .. step:: Contact MongoDB Support if the prompt persists.

Account Suspended for Inactivity
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

|cloudgov-short| suspends accounts after a period of inactivity:

- 35 consecutive days for password and MFA accounts.

- 90 consecutive days for federated accounts.

To restore access to a suspended account, follow the recovery steps
in :ref:`cloudgov-access`.

Verify the Resolution
---------------------

You have resolved the issue when you can log in at
``https://account.mongodbgov.com`` and access your organization.

.. _troubleshoot-diagnostics:

Diagnostics to Collect
----------------------

When you contact MongoDB Support, include the following
information:

- Your organization name.

- The affected username or email address.

- The approximate date and time of the issue, including your time
  zone.

- The text of any error messages.

.. warning::

   Never include sensitive information in case-related emails, such
   as passwords, |api| keys, private keys, or connection strings
   that contain credentials. Case notification emails come from a
   no-reply address that MongoDB does not monitor. To follow up on
   a case, use the |cloudgov-short| Support Portal.

Support Escalation
------------------

If you cannot resolve the issue with the guidance on this page:

- Contact your organization owner. Organization owners can open
  support cases at the organization level.

- Open a case through the |cloudgov-short| Support Portal. To learn
  more, see :ref:`support`.
