=============================
Change a Cluster's Generation
=============================

.. meta::
   :description: Learn how to migrate from a Gen1 cluster to a Gen2 cluster on AWS or Google Cloud.

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

.. include:: /includes/cluster-settings/gen2-clusters/gen2-cluster-intro.rst

You can switch an eligible cluster between Gen1 and Gen2 the same way you
change your cluster's tier.

When you switch from a Gen1 cluster to a Gen2 cluster, |service| maintains your
Gen1 storage capacity and |iops| rate. For clusters hosted on |aws|, |service|
also maintains your :ref:`IOPS type <storage-iops-ui>`.

This can result in your Gen2 cluster having a higher |iops| rate than the
baseline included with your storage capacity. After switching to Gen2, we
recommend you adjust your storage capacity and |iops| rate independently to
fit your workload and optimize your costs. To learn more, see
:ref:`Gen2 Cluster Billing <gen2-clusters-billing>`.

When you switch from a Gen2 cluster to a Gen1 cluster, |service| still
maintains your storage capacity, |iops| rate, and
:ref:`IOPS type <storage-iops-ui>`. However, if your Gen2 cluster's |iops|
rate exceeds the corresponding Gen1 cluster |iops| rate limit, |service|
reduces the |iops| rate to match the Gen1 limit. We recommend you validate
that the Gen1 |iops| are sufficient for your workload requirements before
changing your cluster from Gen2 to Gen1.

You can also use reactive auto-scaling to automatically scale your cluster
between generation-agnostic ``M10`` or ``M20`` clusters and Gen2 clusters.
To learn more, see
:ref:`Scaling a Gen2 Dedicated Cluster <reactive-autoscaling-gen2-cluster>`.

Setting the Cluster Generation Using the {+atlas-ui+}
-----------------------------------------------------

To change you cluster's generation using the
{+atlas-ui+}, select either the :guilabel:`Gen2` or :guilabel:`Gen1` option
under the :guilabel:`Cluster Tier` section.

You can select these options only if your cluster configuration supports Gen2
clusters. If the :guilabel:`Gen2` option is grayed out, your cluster is 
Gen1 by default. To learn more, see:

- :ref:`AWS Gen2 Clusters Reference <aws-reference-gen2-clusters>`
- :ref:`GCP Gen2 Clusters Reference <gcp-reference-gen2-clusters>`

Changing Cluster Generation for GCP-hosted Clusters
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

Clusters hosted on |gcp| use different disk types depending on their
cluster generation:

- **Gen1**, ``M10``, and ``M20`` clusters use Persistent Disk storage.
- **Gen2** clusters use Hyperdisk storage.

To learn more, see :gcp:`Storage Options </compute/docs/disks/>` in the
Google documentation.

Switching a |gcp|-hosted cluster between Gen1 and Gen2 requires |service| to
move your data across these disks, which may take longer than changing the
cluster's tier within a generation.
