===========================
|cloudgov-short| Overview
===========================

.. meta::
   :description: Explore MongoDB Atlas for Government, a fully-managed cloud database for U.S. federal government agencies and their suppliers running sensitive and high-impact workloads.

.. default-domain:: mongodb

|cloudgov| is a fully-managed cloud database for U.S. federal
government agencies and their suppliers.

|cloudgov-short| is certified at FedRAMP® Class D (formerly called
FedRAMP® High).
