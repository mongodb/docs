.. step:: Add an export policy to your {+Cloud-Backup+} schedule.

   .. include:: /includes/backup/export-auto-policy-intro.rst

   To add an export policy:

   a. In the :guilabel:`Backup Policy` tab, go to the
      :guilabel:`Snapshot Export Policy` section.

   #. Under :guilabel:`Export Schedule`, click
      :guilabel:`+ Add Export Schedule`.

   #. From the :guilabel:`Snapshots` dropdown, select the frequency of
      the snapshots that you want to export.

   #. From the :guilabel:`Export Buckets` dropdown, select the export
      bucket that you want to use.

   #. Click :guilabel:`Review Changes`.

   #. Click :guilabel:`Confirm` to apply your changes.
