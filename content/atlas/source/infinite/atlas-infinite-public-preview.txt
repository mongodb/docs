====================================================
MongoDB Atlas Infinite Public Preview Considerations
====================================================

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. facet::
   :name: genre
   :values: reference

.. meta::
   :keywords: atlas infinite, atlas infinite public preview, preview terms, preview considerations
   :description: Learn about the terms and considerations that apply to MongoDB Atlas Infinite during its public preview, including stability, support, and general availability plans.

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

{+atlas-infinite-full+} is offered as a Public Preview while its
feature set expands toward general availability. Each feature released
in this preview is built and tested to the same standards MongoDB
applies to its generally available products for security, data
durability, operational availability, and performance.

The considerations in the `Preview Features
<https://www.mongodb.com/docs/preview-features/>`__ do not apply to
{+atlas-infinite-full+}. The terms on this page apply instead.

Considerations
--------------

- Features available in this preview are listed under
  :ref:`public preview availability <atlas-infinite-availability>`.

- APIs and features released in this preview are stable.
  MongoDB does not intend to make breaking changes to them during
  the preview or at general availability.

- MongoDB intends to make {+atlas-infinite-full+} generally available.

- The measures that apply to {+atlas-infinite-full+} are described in
  the documentation and in the `Technical and Organizational Security
  Measures
  <https://www.mongodb.com/legal/customer-service-agreement/technical-and-organizational-security-measures/>`__.

- Support for {+atlas-infinite-full+} is available through the standard
  MongoDB support channels on a commercially reasonable efforts basis.
  The `MongoDB Atlas Service Level Agreement
  <https://www.mongodb.com/legal/sla/cloud/atlas-database/>`__ and the
  `Cloud Support Policy
  <https://www.mongodb.com/legal/support-policy/cloud/>`__ do not apply
  to this preview.

- Your use of {+atlas-infinite-full+} remains subject to your agreement
  with MongoDB, including its terms for preview offerings.

We recommend that you evaluate whether {+atlas-infinite-full+} is
appropriate for your specific use case. If your workload requires
contractual availability commitments, contact your MongoDB account
team to discuss your needs.
