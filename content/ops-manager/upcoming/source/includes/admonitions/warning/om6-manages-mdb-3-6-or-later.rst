.. warning:: Upgrade Managed Databases to MongoDB 4.0 or Later

   |onprem| 6.0 doesn't support MongoDB 3.6 and earlier. If you are using
   MongoDB 3.6 or earlier and want to upgrade to |onprem| 6.0, you must
   upgrade to at least MongoDB 4.0. However, we recommend that you upgrade
   to at least MongoDB 4.2 before upgrading to |onprem| 6.0.
