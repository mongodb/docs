Atlas Roles for Project Management

Overview
--------

In this guide, you can learn about the Atlas roles required to perform
agent and project management tasks on the {+platform-short+}.
Your Atlas role determines your access to both your {+platform-short+}
settings and the agents in a project.

Your ability to manage the agents in a project depends on your Atlas
project role, not your organization role.

Full Access Role Requirement
~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~~

For full access to {+platform-short+} settings and agent management,
you must have the ``Project Owner`` role on the Atlas project that
contains the agent.

.. important:: Organization Owner Limitations

   The ``Organization Owner`` role alone does not grant access to
   agent management. Atlas uses a hierarchical permission
   model, so the ``Organization Owner`` role grants implicit
   ``Project Owner`` access to every project in the organization.
   Currently, the {+platform-short+} doesn't use this model.
   Instead, the platform checks for an explicit ``Project Owner``
   assignment on the project.

Role Access
-----------

Atlas roles grant access to two separate resources: your
organization's or project's {+platform-short+} settings, and the
agents themselves. The following table describes the Atlas roles and
their access to {+platform-short+} resources:

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 25 25 50

   * - Atlas Role
     - Access
     - Available Actions

   * - ``Project Owner``
     - | Read and write access to agents
       | Read and write access to settings
     - | You can perform all agent management tasks, including the following actions:
       |
       | * Create, edit, pause, resume, and delete agents
       | * View agent logs and data
       | * Manage project access
       | * Create Atlas clusters
       | * Manage triggers and workflows
       |
       | You can also view and change {+platform-short+} settings.

   * - All other Atlas project roles, such as ``Project Read Only``
       and ``Project Cluster Creator``
     - | Read-only access to agents
       | Read-only access to settings
     - | You can view agents, view agent logs, and
         read agent data. You cannot create, edit, pause, resume, or
         delete an agent.
       | You can also view {+platform-short+} settings, but you
         cannot change them.

   * - ``Organization Owner``
     - Read and write access to settings
     - You can view and change {+platform-short+} settings for the
       organization, and you can enable the {+platform-short+} on a project.
       You do not have agent management access within that project.

   * - All other Atlas organization roles, such as ``Organization
       Group Creator`` and  ``Organization Read Only``
     - Read-only access to settings
     - You can view {+platform-short+} settings for the organization,
       but you cannot change them. You do not have agent management
       access.
