General Changes
---------------

Server Status
~~~~~~~~~~~~~

MongoDB 9.1 adds the following :dbcommand:`serverStatus` metrics:

- :serverstatus:`metrics.query.out.countFailedOverQuota`
- :serverstatus:`metrics.query.out.countInvokedWithQuota`
