October 2026
------------

- Announces that |cloudgov| is certified at FedRAMP® Class D
  (formerly called FedRAMP® High). This certification covers
  |aws-fr-high| (US) regions, |gaw| (US) regions, and |azure-gov|
  (US) regions. To learn more, see :ref:`cloudgov-overview` and
  :ref:`security`.

- General Availability: Adds |azure-gov| as a cloud provider for
  |cloudgov-short|. You can deploy clusters in the USGov Virginia,
  USGov Texas, and USGov Arizona regions. Note that |azure-gov|
  regions support only ``M30`` or higher cluster tiers,
  and |service| Stream Processing remains unavailable in
  |cloudgov-short|. To learn more, see :ref:`supported-regions`.
