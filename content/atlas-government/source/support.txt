.. _support:

=======
Support
=======

.. meta::
   :description: Access premium support for MongoDB Atlas for Government with guaranteed US-based assistance and specific user roles for raising support cases.

.. default-domain:: mongodb

.. contents:: On this page
   :local:
   :backlinks: none
   :depth: 1
   :class: singlecol

Your |cloudgov| subscription entitles you to a premium support plan.

Platform SLA and Support Terms
------------------------------

|cloudgov| includes the same 99.995% uptime Service Level Agreement
(SLA) as commercial |service|. The SLA is a platform availability
commitment. [#sla-fault-domains]_

MongoDB supports |cloudgov| projects with US persons on US soil.
Support operates under Initial Response Goals (IRGs). To learn more
about support terms, including severity levels and response goals,
see the `MongoDB Atlas for Government Support Policy
<https://www.mongodb.com/legal/support-policy/government/>`__.

Request Support
---------------

Open a support case against a project:

1. Select your organization from the :guilabel:`Organizations` menu in
   the navigation bar.

#. Select your project from the :guilabel:`Projects` menu in
   the navigation bar.

#. Next to the :guilabel:`Projects` menu, expand the
   :guilabel:`Options` menu, then click :guilabel:`Project Support`.

.. _support-email-communications:

Support Email Communications
----------------------------

The following guidelines apply to email communications from
|cloudgov-short| and MongoDB Support:

- |cloudgov| application emails, including account and case
  notifications, come from the ``mongodbgov.com`` domain.

- Case notification emails come from a no-reply address that
  MongoDB does not monitor. To follow up on a case, use the
  `AtlasGov Support Portal <https://cloud.mongodbgov.com>`__.

- Do not include sensitive information in case-related emails, such
  as passwords, |api| keys, private keys, or connection strings
  that contain credentials.

- When you open or follow up on a case, collect the information
  listed in :ref:`Diagnostics to Collect
  <troubleshoot-diagnostics>` to help MongoDB Support investigate.


.. _support-required-roles:

Required Roles
--------------

To raise and view support cases, you must have the appropriate role:

.. tip::

   |cloudgov-short| user roles are the same as
   :atlas:`Atlas User Roles </reference/user-roles/>`.

.. list-table::
   :header-rows: 1
   :widths: 20 20 60

   * - Support Cases
     - Scope
     - Role
   * - Can View
     - Project
     - Any user with any role in the project
   * - Can Raise
     - Project
     - All user with any project role except the
       :authrole:`Project Read Only` role
   * - Can View and Raise
     - All projects in the organization
     - :authrole:`Organization Owner`

Users can access only projects to which they have been added. Any
user who can raise a case can also view cases.
:authrole:`Organization Owners <Organization Owner>` have access to
all projects in the organization by default.

.. [#sla-fault-domains]

   In |azure-gov| regions, |service| deploys clusters across two
   fault domains rather than across Availability Zones. Clusters
   in two-fault-domain regions have a higher chance of losing
   availability than clusters in regions with three fault domains.
   To learn more, see :ref:`atlasgov-azure-government`.
